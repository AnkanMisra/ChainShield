ChainShield Agent — policy-bound risk gate

Getting started — 30 second walkthrough

  1. Click Quick demo in the Policies section. This creates a sample policy for treasury 0x1111…1111 with caps 1 ETH/tx, 3 ETH/day, allowlist [0x2222…2222], and forbids the ERC-20 approve selector.
  2. Scroll to Evaluate transaction intent, pick the new policy in the dropdown, then click any preset:
ALLOWSafe transfer — sends 0.5 ETH to the allowlisted vault. Should approve, score 0.
BLOCKOver-cap — tries to send 5 ETH (above 1 ETH/tx cap). Risk 90, blocked.
BLOCKForbidden approve — submits an unlimited approve(attacker, MAX_UINT256). Risk 95, blocked.
CONFIRMUnknown destination — sends 0.1 ETH to an off-allowlist address. Risk 60, requires human confirmation.
  1. Click Evaluate. The verdict appears with score, reasons, and a raw JSON dump.
  2. Open the Incident timeline at the bottom — every decision is appended in order.

Hex-encoded EVM calldata. 0x for plain ETH transfers. The first 4 bytes (0x + 8 hex chars) are the function selector — that's what forbidden selectors match against.
